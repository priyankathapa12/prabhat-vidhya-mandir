About Our School

Prabhat Vidhya Mandir is a private school located in Barahakshetra Municipality, Sunsari, Koshi Province. Established in 2061 B.S., our school is committed to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined and caring environment.

According to the 2081 IEMIS report, the school has 121 students enrolled from ECD to Grade 7.

Message from the Principal

Dear Students, Parents, and Well-Wishers,

Welcome to Prabhat Vidhya Mandir. Since our establishment in 2061 B.S., we have been committed to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined, and inspiring learning environment.

At our school, every child is encouraged to discover their talents, develop confidence, and become a responsible citizen. We believe education is the foundation of success and character.

Together with our dedicated teachers and supportive parents, we continue to nurture academic excellence, creativity, leadership, and strong moral values.

I sincerely thank all parents and well-wishers for their continuous trust and support. We look forward to welcoming every child into our school family.
